Why service history can raise your car's resale value

Learn why complete car service records improve buyer confidence, support resale value and make a used-car sale easier.

Records tell the ownership story

Invoices and authorised or trusted-workshop records show when fluids, filters, brakes and major components were serviced. They help a buyer distinguish responsible ownership from a car prepared only for sale.

Follow time as well as distance

Cars with low annual mileage still need time-based maintenance. Fluids age, batteries weaken and rubber components deteriorate. Following the manufacturer schedule produces a clearer record and reduces avoidable failures.

Document repairs honestly

Accident or mechanical repairs should be explained with invoices where possible. Transparent evidence can build more confidence than vague assurances that a car has never needed work.

Make the history easy to review

Keep physical invoices in date order and maintain digital copies. Add insurance, tyre, battery and pollution records. A buyer who can understand the car quickly is less likely to negotiate from uncertainty.

Important: Vehicle condition, finance and RTO requirements vary. Verify the specific car, lender terms and applicable government process before committing.
